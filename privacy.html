PRIVACY POLICY

Updated 15 October 2026

At INTERLUDE, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our store in BGC or use our digital services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you interact with us:

  • Identity Data: Full name, username, or similar identifiers.
  • Contact Data: Email address, billing address, delivery address, and mobile number.
  • Transaction Data: Details about payments and products you purchased from us.

How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 for the following purposes:

  • To fulfill and deliver your product orders.
  • To manage your customer account and process payments.
  • To send marketing communications, updates, or promotional offers (only with your explicit consent).

Data Retention and Security

Your personal data is stored securely and is only retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

Your Rights under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject in the Philippines, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal data.
  • Object to processing or request the erasure of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you feel your rights have been violated.

For any privacy concerns, contact our Data Protection Officer at interlude.privacy@gmail.com.
